Our Story

Shaping Leaders Since 1975

Founded in 1975, EduVerse College began as a small engineering school with just 3 departments and 200 students. Today we are a full-fledged university offering 150+ programs across 20+ disciplines, serving over 15,000 students from 40+ countries.

Our journey has been defined by a relentless pursuit of academic excellence, groundbreaking research, and a deep commitment to student welfare. We believe education is not merely instruction — it is transformation.

Core Pillars

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality education that empowers students with knowledge, skills, and values to excel in a rapidly evolving world and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognised institution of academic excellence, research innovation, and holistic development — producing graduates who lead with integrity and impact.

Our Values

Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, Inclusivity, and Social Responsibility form the bedrock of everything we do — from curriculum design to campus culture.

Milestones

Our Journey

1975

Foundation

EduVerse College established with 3 departments and 200 students in New Delhi.

1990

University Status

Granted deemed university status by UGC. Management and Sciences faculties added.

2005

NAAC Accreditation

Received NAAC 'A' grade accreditation. Research centres established across 10 departments.

2015

Global Partnerships

Signed MoUs with 50+ international universities. International student enrolment doubled.

2022

NAAC A+ & NIRF Top 10

Upgraded to NAAC 'A+'. Ranked in NIRF Top 10 engineering institutions nationally.

2025

AI & Innovation Hub

Launched dedicated AI Research Hub in collaboration with Google, Microsoft, and ISRO.
